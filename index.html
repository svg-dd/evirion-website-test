Evirion product walkthroughFormation sequence / 01–04
Formation sequence01-04
01 / Observe

Reasoning starts scattered across real work.

A merged pull request, review constraint, and referenced incident enter separately. They are evidence, not memory.

02 / Extract

A candidate forms without losing provenance.

Decision, rationale, and constraint assemble into one object while every claim remains attached to its source.

03 / Review

Fluency never substitutes for authority.

A qualified reviewer checks evidence support, decision authority, and lifecycle before an explicit verdict.

04 / Retrieve

Reviewed context returns when it is needed.

External fragments disappear. Provenance stays inside the admitted record and supports the recalled answer.
