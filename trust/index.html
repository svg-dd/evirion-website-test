Trust architecture

Confidence needs a chain of custody.

Trust comes from inspectable evidence, explicit admission states, and clear human authority, not model fluency.Security posture is described without certification or deployment claims the pilot has not earned.

Every trusted record answers three questions.

PROVENANCE

Where did this come from?

Source reference, author, time, and quoted evidence remain attached.

AUTHORITY

Who decided to trust it?

A reviewer controls admission and leaves a visible state transition.

TEMPORAL VALIDITY

When should it be reconsidered?

Validity conditions and uncertainty stay part of the record.

Follow the record back to evidence.

What the pilot controls, and what it does not claim.

Controls

Boundaries in the pilot

  • Source access

    Repository access is scoped during operator-led onboarding.

  • Trusted admission

    Only human-accepted candidate records enter trusted memory.

  • Model output

    Generated text is not automatically treated as organizational truth.

Does not claim

Promises not made by this site

  • SOC 2 certification

    No certification claim is made for the current private pilot.

  • Enterprise SLA

    Availability and support terms are agreed per pilot, not advertised as GA.

  • Universal source coverage

    Non-GitHub sources remain planned.

A useful pilot starts with the trust questions your team cannot compromise.
