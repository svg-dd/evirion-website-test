Company thesis

Build systems that remember.

Complex systems should preserve not only their current state, but the decisions and constraints that created it.Evirion starts with a narrow engineering problem and keeps the broader company direction explicitly labelled.

AI can produce and change software faster than organizations can preserve the reasoning that makes those changes safe.

Repositories preserve state. Work trackers preserve fragments. People carry the connections between them. Teams change, systems age, and agents act without the historical context a senior engineer would ask for.

Evirion is built around a simple premise: decision context is part of the system, and it should be treated with the same care as other infrastructure.
