Product · Engineering Decision Memory

Decision memory, with its evidence intact.

Evirion structures decisions, reasons, constraints, rejected alternatives, and source evidence into a record a team can inspect.The private pilot starts with merged GitHub pull requests and operator-led review.

A summary is not enough.

A durable record keeps the anatomy of a decision visible so future readers can evaluate it instead of trusting a fluent paragraph.

Meaning

Decision
Keep retry policy outside the request handler.
Rationale
Centralizing retry policy keeps backoff behavior consistent and prevents request paths from retrying non-idempotent operations.
Constraints
Retries are permitted only for idempotent operations and must respect the caller deadline.
Tradeoffs
Callers gain one shared policy but must declare idempotency before retries are enabled.
Failure mode
Per-handler loops can retry unsafe operations and drift across services.
Alternatives
Per-handler retry loops were rejected because policy drifted across services.

Evidence

Move retry orchestration to the client boundary and leave the request handler single-attempt.

Observe. Extract. Review. Admit. Retrieve.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Read merged engineering evidence without changing source systems.

  2. 02

    Extract

    Form candidate claims with provenance and visible uncertainty.

  3. 03

    Review

    Let a qualified person accept, reject, or quarantine the candidate.

  4. 04

    Admit

    Write only accepted records to trusted memory.

  5. 05

    Retrieve

    Return relevant context with evidence and validity attached.

One memory. Different operating questions.

Engineering leadershipWhich decisions create continuity risk if ownership changes?
Evaluate whether consequential decisions remain understandable as ownership, teams, and systems change.
Platform & DevExWhich operating constraints should every service reuse?
Connect reviewed reasons and constraints to the workflows that repeatedly need them.
Staff & architectureWhy was the rejected design unsafe for this system?
Inspect rejected alternatives, validity conditions, and evidence instead of reconstructing them from fragments.
AI enablementWhich reviewed context is safe to deliver to an agent?
Keep human-reviewed decisions distinct from candidate output before exposing organizational reasoning to coding agents.

Current scope and next sources stay visibly separate.
