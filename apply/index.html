Private design partnerships

Bring us the context your team keeps losing.

An operator-led private pilot for engineering organizations with consequential, long-lived systems and a concrete decision-retrieval problem.Applications are evaluated for fit. This is not a self-service trial or a promise of immediate access.

A useful pilot has a real question.

  • History matters

    Your codebase is old or consequential enough that rationale changes how work should proceed.

  • Evidence exists

    Merged GitHub pull requests contain meaningful review and decision context.

  • A team will review

    A qualified engineering partner can evaluate candidate records with us.

  • AI raises the stakes

    Coding agents or faster change volume make missing context more expensive.

Small scope. Explicit evaluation.

  1. 01

    Fit conversation

    Clarify the context problem, source history, constraints, and evaluation owner.

  2. 02

    Scoped onboarding

    Agree repository access, handling boundaries, and the initial decision sample.

  3. 03

    Candidate review

    Evaluate extraction quality and trust states with a qualified engineering reviewer.

  4. 04

    Decision

    Document what worked, what failed, and whether a broader engagement is justified.
