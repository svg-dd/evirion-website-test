Legal · Privacy

Privacy and data handling.

Effective August 23, 2026. Evirion collects application information, limited anti-abuse signals, and privacy-oriented aggregate website analytics.

Application data

We collect the name, work email, company, role, engineering-team range, coding-agent usage, and context problem that an applicant chooses to submit.

Purpose

Application details are used only to evaluate design-partnership fit, operate the intake process, prevent abuse, and respond to the applicant. They are not sold or used to train models.

Service providers

Cloudflare hosts the site, verifies Turnstile challenges, and stores application records in D1. Resend delivers the internal application notification. These providers process data to provide those services.

Anti-abuse and analytics

A salted, one-way network identifier is used for short-window rate limiting; raw addresses are not stored in the application record. Cloudflare Web Analytics provides aggregate site measurement without application-form values.

Retention

The D1 application record receives a deletion date 365 days after submission. It may be removed sooner when no longer needed, while separate correspondence may be retained when required for an active relationship or legal obligation. Short-lived rate-limit records are operational data and are eligible for deletion after one day.

Requests

Applicants may request access, correction, or deletion using the privacy address below. We may need to verify the requester before acting. privacy@evirion.ai
